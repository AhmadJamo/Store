# Purchases
> Status: IMPLEMENTED | Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

Creates a purchase header/items after validating supplier, each line's warehouse, active product, quantity and non-negative price. Prepared-to-order products are excluded because their ingredients, not the menu item, are purchased. One invoice can distribute products across warehouses. Exact rack/bin is not requested, so receipts enter Unassigned Stock. A line total is quantity × price minus fixed line discount; its net unit cost updates the target product/warehouse moving average inside the same UnitOfWork and the purchase movement freezes the full valuation change. Current recoverable tax remains outside inventory cost.

From purchase details, a permitted user can post once to the ledger. Posting uses each line's warehouse inventory account and dimension, plus input tax, purchase discount and supplier payable accounts. The supplier invoice number remains an external reference; the generated journal entry receives its own centralized JournalEntry sequence number in the posting transaction. Historical items inherit their header warehouse. Sources: `Purchase`, `PurchaseItem`; Purchases DTOs; `PurchaseService`, `PurchasePostingService`; repositories/configurations and purchase screens. No return/cancel/reversal is implemented.
