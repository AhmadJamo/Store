# Purchases
> Status: IMPLEMENTED | Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

Creates a purchase header/items after validating supplier, each line's warehouse, active product, quantity and non-negative price. Prepared-to-order products are excluded because their ingredients, not the menu item, are purchased. One invoice can distribute products across warehouses. Exact rack/bin is not requested, so receipts enter Unassigned Stock. A line total is quantity × price minus fixed line discount; its net unit cost updates the target product/warehouse moving average inside the same UnitOfWork and the purchase movement freezes the full valuation change. Recoverable input tax is excluded from inventory cost, including tax extracted from price-inclusive lines.

From purchase details, a permitted user can post once to the ledger. Posting uses each line's warehouse inventory account and dimension, plus input tax, purchase discount and supplier payable accounts. Receipt movements with provisional-negative cost variance add a COGS/inventory adjustment in the correct debit/credit direction for each affected warehouse. The supplier invoice number remains an external reference; the generated journal entry receives its own centralized JournalEntry sequence number in the posting transaction. Historical items inherit their header warehouse. Sources: `Purchase`, `PurchaseItem`; Purchases DTOs; `PurchaseService`, `PurchasePostingService`; repositories/configurations and purchase screens. No return/cancel/reversal is implemented.
