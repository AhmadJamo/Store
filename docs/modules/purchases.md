# Purchases
> Status: IMPLEMENTED | Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

Creates a purchase header/items after validating supplier, each line's warehouse, product, quantity and non-negative price. One supplier invoice can distribute products across multiple warehouses; the header warehouse is only a convenient default for new lines. Exact rack/bin is intentionally not requested during purchase entry, so received quantities enter the Unassigned Stock queue. If the product's existing balance in the selected warehouse is zero, purchase creation first removes its obsolete rack/bin allocations; the new receipt therefore appears in full in Unassigned Stock. A line may contain a fixed discount amount and optional tax rate; its total is quantity × price minus discount. Inside UnitOfWork it creates/updates each target warehouse balance and writes the purchase movement.

From purchase details, a permitted user can post once to the ledger. Posting uses each line's warehouse inventory account and dimension, plus input tax, purchase discount and supplier payable accounts. Historical items inherit their header warehouse. Sources: `Purchase`, `PurchaseItem`; Purchases DTOs; `PurchaseService`, `PurchasePostingService`; repositories/configurations and purchase screens. No return/cancel/reversal is implemented.
