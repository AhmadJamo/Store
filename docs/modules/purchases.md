# Purchases
> Status: IMPLEMENTED | Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

Creates a purchase header/items after validating supplier, warehouse, product, quantity and non-negative price. A line may contain a fixed discount amount and an optional tax rate; its total is quantity × price minus discount. Inside UnitOfWork it creates/updates product stock and creates a Purchase stock transaction using supplier invoice number as reference. ProductStock rowversion detects concurrent writes, and the aggregate rejects duplicate product lines. There are List/Create/Details screens; no edit/delete actions despite permissions being defined.

From the purchase details page, a permitted user can post once to the general ledger. Posting requires the supplier payable account and warehouse inventory account; a discounted purchase also requires the configured purchase-discount account. The entry debits inventory and input tax, credits the purchase discount where applicable, and credits the supplier payable account. The journal source uniqueness constraint prevents duplicate posting. Sources: `Purchase`, `PurchaseItem`; Purchases DTOs; `PurchaseService`, `PurchasePostingService`; repository/interface/configurations; `PurchasesController`; `Views/Purchases/{Index,Create,Details}.cshtml`. Permissions: Purchases.View/Create. No return/cancel/reversal is implemented.
