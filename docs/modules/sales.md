# Sales
> Status: IMPLEMENTED | Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

Wholesale Create and Retail POS call `SalesController.Create` then `SaleService.CreateAsync`. Service validates configured discount rules/limits, generates number from `InvoiceSettings`, reads product stock, chooses server-side wholesale or retail product price, adds sale items, decreases stock and writes Sale movement rows in one UnitOfWork transaction. ProductStock rowversion detects concurrent writes, and the aggregate rejects duplicate product lines. User-provided item `SalePrice` is ignored.

Sales and POS require a payment method, which maps the cash/bank/card settlement to a chart account. A registered customer uses a dedicated subaccount and may be selected; leaving it blank records an unknown walk-in customer. Configured sales discounts have a central sales-discount account, while revenue and COGS accounts are configured centrally. Sources: Sale/SaleItem/SaleChannel/DiscountType; Sales DTOs; `ISaleService`, `SaleService`, `PaymentMethodService`; invoice/discount repositories; `SalesController`; `Views/Sales/{Index,Create,Details,Pos}.cshtml`; sale/payment EF configurations. Permissions: Sales.View/Create. Returns, tax and journal posting are not implemented.
